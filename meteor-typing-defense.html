🛈 How to Play

  • Meteors fall from the top, each carrying a word.
  • Type it exactly and it fires automatically — no Enter needed.
  • Matching letters glow blue as you type — that's your locked-on target.
  • A meteor that reaches the dashed line hits your castle and costs a life.
  • You start with 3 lives — lose them all and it's game over.
  • Longer words score more points, but the sky gets faster and busier as your score climbs.
  • A wrong guess just flashes red — no penalty, try again.
